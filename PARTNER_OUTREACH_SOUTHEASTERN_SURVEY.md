## The Workflow (Simple)

1. **You get a job** that involves aerial data (site assessment, progress monitoring, volumetrics, boundary confirmation)
2. **You send us:** property address, scope, deadline, any special requirements
3. **We fly:** typically within 48 hours of your request
4. **We deliver:** raw data + processed orthomosaic + 3D model + point cloud (LiDAR if needed)
5. **You certify:** integrate it into your survey deliverables, stamp, deliver to client
6. **We invoice you** a flat fee per flight; you bill your client however you want
