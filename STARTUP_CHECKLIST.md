## Risk Mitigation

**What could go wrong + how to handle:**

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Insurance delay | Blocks all flying | Get quotes Day 1, bind by Friday |
| Weather blocks flights | Delays schedule | Have 2-3 backup dates per job |
| Airspace restricted | Can't fly property | Check LAANC before proposing |
| Client not satisfied | Negative feedback | Use quality checklist before delivery |
| Payment not received | Cash flow issue | Follow up at day 15 + 30 |
| Equipment failure | Job incomplete | Have backup battery + charger |
| Quota/acres miscalculated | Margin too low | Test calculator with sample jobs |
