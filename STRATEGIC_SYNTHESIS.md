### PHASE 2: Operationalize Stage 1 (After Gate Clears)
**Goal:** Land first recurring contract. 3+ jobs/month consistently. Build operational leverage.

**Build:**
1. **CRM / Lead Pipeline Tracker** (HubSpot free tier or Notion database)
   - Track: every lead, every touchpoint, every opportunity
   - Trigger: automated follow-up reminders
   - Value: You'll never miss a sale just because you forgot to follow up

2. **Automated Invoice & Payment System** (Square, Stripe, or QuickBooks)
   - Auto-generate invoices from job template
   - Send payment reminders
   - Track payment status in one view
   - Value: Cash flow visibility, less manual work

3. **Scheduling & Booking System** (Calendly or built into site)
   - Clients can self-schedule (select date, time, service)
   - Auto-sends confirmation + service agreement
   - Value: You don't have to go back-and-forth on scheduling

4. **Flight Log Automation** (Google Form → Sheets or Zapier)
   - Post-flight: fill form (property, acres, feedback, amount)
   - Auto-populates dashboard
   - Value: Data capture is frictionless

5. **Partner Portal / White-Label Version** (simple Notion page or iframe)
   - Partners see their jobs, status, deliverables
   - Auto-updates when you upload data
   - Value: Partners track their own work; you don't have to update them

---

### PHASE 3: Platform-Ready (Stage 2-3, 6+ Months Out)
**Goal:** Property history database + city monitoring + self-serve owner portal

**Build:**
1. **Property Database** (Airtable or Notion)
   - Every property: aerial scans, 3D models, history, change over time
   - Link: before/after images, reports, recommendations
   - Query: "show me all commercial properties we've scanned in Valdosta"
   - Value: The data becomes your moat. Recurring monitoring becomes obvious.

2. **Self-Serve Owner Portal** (Webflow, Carrd, or custom)
   - Homeowner enters address
   - Sees: "Get your property scanned" with 3 price tiers
   - Automatic scheduling, payment, reporting
   - Value: You stop being the bottleneck; customers find you + buy directly

3. **City Monitoring Dashboard** (Tableau, Metabase, or custom)
   - Real-time view: properties scanned in Valdosta, trends, heat maps
   - Pitch to city: "Here's your neighborhood in 3D, updated every quarter"
   - Value: New revenue stream (city contracts), justifies recurring flights
