1. **Your client needs an audit** (ESA, wetland assessment, habitat survey, etc.)
2. **You offer the aerial option:** "We can add aerial documentation for $X. Gives you comprehensive site coverage + change-over-time tracking."
3. **Client says yes:** you send us the address, scope, deadline
4. **We fly:** typically within 48 hours
5. **We deliver:** orthomosaic, 3D model, point cloud, basic analysis
