Script Library 💕

Interoception Awareness

Building awareness:

“I’m working on noticing what my body is telling me. It would help if you gently ask ‘How are you feeling in your body right now?’”
When struggling to read signals:

“I’m having trouble reading my internal signals right now (interoception). I need a few minutes to check in with my body. I’ll let you know when I understand what I need.”
To your partner:

“My brain sometimes has difficulty sensing hunger, tiredness, or rising anxiety. When I seem off, a gentle check-in helps me a lot.”

Secure Attachment Healing

Asking for a need securely:

“I’ve been feeling a bit disconnected lately. Can we plan some quality time this week?”
