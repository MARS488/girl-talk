# Property Access & Flight Authorization Form

**Signed by the property owner before every flight. Confirms they own/control the property and are giving explicit permission to access it and fly a drone over it. This is separate from the Service Agreement — the agreement covers the business deal; this form covers the physical access and flight itself, and is the document that matters most if a neighbor or authority ever asks "who said you could fly here."**

## What I'm authorizing

By signing below, I confirm and authorize the following:

1. **I own this property, or I am authorized to grant access and flight permission on the owner's behalf.** (If a renter, property manager, or HOA representative — written authority from the actual owner may be required before the flight if requested.)
2. I authorize CivilitySync / Marcus Jay Herring LLC, operating under FAA Part 107 certificate #5051412, to **access this property** for the purpose of launching, flying, and landing an unmanned aircraft (drone) to capture aerial imagery and/or data on: ___________ (date), or during the window: ___________ (date range).
3. I understand this authorization covers **this property only** — it does not authorize flying over or photographing neighboring properties, and CivilitySync will make reasonable efforts to avoid doing so.
4. I understand the flight will be conducted under FAA rules (daylight/twilight hours, visual line of sight, altitude limits, weather permitting) and may be rescheduled for weather, mechanical, or airspace-authorization reasons at no additional cost to me.
5. I have been given the opportunity to ask questions about the flight, and understand what will be captured and why (see Service Agreement / quote for scope).
6. **Data ownership and use** is governed by the Service Agreement for this job — this form only authorizes the physical access and flight, not a separate data license.

## What I am NOT authorizing

- Flights beyond visual line of sight, at night, or over uninvolved people, unless a separate FAA waiver is disclosed to me in writing.
- Access to the property for any purpose other than the flight described above.
- Any physical alteration of the property (this is aerial documentation only — no ground work is performed under this form).

Keep the signed copy with the job file per the record-keeping list in the vault's Launch Readiness note (§12).*

*This is a plain-English starting template, not legal advice — before relying on it for real liability protection, have a Georgia attorney review it once alongside the Service Agreement and Liability Waiver (often inexpensive as a single small-business document review).*
