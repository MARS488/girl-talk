# Operations Checklist — Marcus Jay Herring LLC

**Everything that needs to be in place before paid work can happen. Use as your readiness checklist.**

---

## Phase 1: Legal Foundation (Before Any Paid Work)

### LLC & Business Structure
- [ ] LLC formation document filed with Georgia Secretary of State
- [ ] Operating agreement created (even simple one-pager)
- [ ] EIN assigned (IRS Form SS-4)
- [ ] Business bank account opened (bring EIN letter + LLC docs)
- [ ] Business phone number/email established

### Insurance (Non-Negotiable)
- [ ] General liability policy bound (min $1M/$2M)
- [ ] Drone-specific liability policy bound (min $1M/$2M)
- [ ] Certificates of insurance issued + kept on hand
- [ ] Client can be named as additional insured (know how to request)

### Compliance & Credentials
- [ ] FAA Part 107 license current (or scheduled for exam)
- [ ] Drone(s) registered with FAA (tail numbers visible)
- [ ] Remote ID enabled and compliant on all aircraft
- [ ] Recurrent training deadline on calendar (every 2 years)
- [ ] Local airspace rules understood (LAANC familiar)

---

## Phase 2: Operations & Safety

### Flight Operations
- [ ] Pre-flight checklist created and printed
- [ ] Flight log template ready (date, location, duration, notes)
- [ ] Weather/wind limits documented (for your equipment)
- [ ] Backup battery + charger (car charging if field work)
- [ ] Spare propellers, SD cards, ND filters on hand
- [ ] Firmware update process documented (test before jobs)
- [ ] GPS/airspace check routine before every flight
- [ ] Data backup system (cloud + local) for all imagery

### Safety & Site Management
- [ ] Personal protective equipment (PPE) for job sites
- [ ] Site hazard assessment routine (power lines, terrain, people)
- [ ] Emergency contact/first aid kit in vehicle
- [ ] Incident/near-miss reporting process defined

---

## Phase 3: Sales & Legal Documents

### Sales Materials
- [ ] Service agreement template customized (your LLC name + terms)
- [ ] Partnership proposal framework ready (for B2B outreach)
- [ ] Sample deliverables prepared (best previous work or demo)
- [ ] Pitch one-liner polished ("what do you do?" answer)
- [ ] Proof of insurance available to send clients

### Pricing & Quotes
- [ ] Quote calculator running (know costs + margins)
- [ ] Pricing tiers set for your service(s) (2-3 tiers)
- [ ] Deposit policy clear (for jobs with hard costs)
- [ ] Revision policy documented (what's included?)
- [ ] Payment methods set up (PayPal / Square / Stripe)

### Legal Setup
- [ ] Service agreement signed before every job
- [ ] W-9 process understood (collect from business clients)
- [ ] Confidentiality / NDA terms drafted (if needed for verticals)
- [ ] Liability waivers / indemnification clauses finalized

---

## Phase 4: Finance & Bookkeeping

### Accounting System
- [ ] Bookkeeping software chosen (Sheets, Wave, QB, etc.)
- [ ] Invoice template created
- [ ] Chart of accounts set up (income + expense categories)
- [ ] Monthly reconciliation process documented

### Income & Invoicing
- [ ] Invoice numbering system established
- [ ] Payment terms documented (net 15? net 30? upfront?)
- [ ] Payment collection process (follow-up on overdue invoices)
- [ ] Receipt tracking system (scan or photo all receipts)
- [ ] Monthly income summary routine

### Expenses & Mileage
- [ ] Mileage tracking method chosen (app or logbook)
- [ ] Expense categories defined (batteries, software, travel, insurance, etc.)
- [ ] Monthly expense review routine
- [ ] Receipts organized by month

### Taxes & Quarterly Planning
- [ ] Estimated tax rate calculated (talk to CPA)
- [ ] Quarterly payment schedule on calendar (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15)
- [ ] Tax set-aside account opened (separate from operating account)
- [ ] Year-end tax prep timeline planned
- [ ] CPA/bookkeeper identified (even if not hired yet)

---

## Phase 5: Customer Operations

### Scheduling & Delivery
- [ ] Scheduling system chosen (calendar app, spreadsheet, Calendly)
- [ ] Lead capture method defined (how do customers find you?)
- [ ] Job intake form created (captures all necessary info)
- [ ] Turnaround time defined and communicated (48 hrs? 1 week?)
- [ ] Delivery method chosen (Dropbox, Google Drive, email, USB drive)

### Quality Standards
- [ ] Deliverable template created (report format, naming, sections)
- [ ] Quality checklist for every job (before delivery)
- [ ] Revision policy communicated (what's included?)
- [ ] Feedback collection process (ask "would you use again?")

---

## Phase 6: Partnerships & Growth

### Partner Identification
- [ ] Primary verticals chosen (e.g., storm/disaster, land stewardship)
- [ ] Warm partners identified (Southeastern Survey, environmental firms, etc.)
- [ ] Marketplace accounts researched (Zeitview, DroneUp, etc.)
- [ ] Local contractor/crew relationships initiated (for sub-outs)

### Partner Outreach
- [ ] Partnership proposals customized for each prospect
- [ ] One-page sell sheet created (easy to hand out)
- [ ] Sample deliverable ready to show
- [ ] First contact process planned (email? phone? coffee?)
- [ ] Follow-up schedule set (remind yourself to follow up)

---

## Phase 7: Tracking & Metrics

### Daily/Weekly
- [ ] Flight log entry made after each job
- [ ] Expense receipt captured immediately
- [ ] Client feedback collected within 48 hours
- [ ] Invoice sent within 24 hours of delivery

### Monthly
- [ ] Income summary calculated
- [ ] Expenses totaled and categorized
- [ ] Bank reconciliation done
- [ ] Dashboard metrics updated
- [ ] Margin analysis run (are you hitting targets?)

### Quarterly
- [ ] Tax set-aside payment made
- [ ] Quarterly review (what's working? what's not?)
- [ ] Partner outreach follow-up (coffee meetings, pilots)
- [ ] Pricing review (are your tiers right?)

---

## Phase 8: Growth Gates (Stage Progression)

### Stage 0 → Stage 1 Gate
Clear when:
- [ ] 3+ properties/jobs completed
- [ ] 2+ clients say "would use again"
- [ ] 1+ paid job (revenue confirmed)
- [ ] Process documented enough that someone else could follow it

### Stage 1 → Stage 2 Gate
Clear when:
- [ ] 3+ paying jobs per month (consistent)
- [ ] At least 1 recurring contract (monthly/seasonal/annual)
- [ ] Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) calculation possible
- [ ] Delegation starting (not flying every job yourself)

---

## The Minimum Viable Setup (To Start Today)

**Don't wait until everything is perfect. You need:**

1. ✅ LLC in good standing + bank account
2. ✅ General + drone insurance bound (non-negotiable)
3. ✅ FAA Part 107 current + drone registered
4. ✅ Service agreement template ready to sign
5. ✅ Quote calculator running (know your costs)
6. ✅ Bookkeeping system started (even if just Sheets)
7. ✅ Sample deliverable ready to show

**That's it.** Everything else can follow. But these 7 things must be in place before taking paid work.

---

## Automation Opportunities (Later)

Once you're running:
- Schedule invoicing reminders
- Automate report generation (templates + fill-ins)
- Webhook-based flight logging
- Automated customer feedback collection (post-delivery email)
- Dashboard auto-updates (API connections)

**But first:** Get the manual version working reliably. Automate what's painful, not what's easy.

---

## Review Frequency

- **Monthly:** Full review of the checklist — which items need attention?
- **Quarterly:** Deeper review — which items are bottlenecks?
- **Annually:** Complete refresh — update dates, dependencies, processes

**Golden rule:** If something stops working, add it to the checklist so it gets caught next time.
