## Step 3: Bind Insurance (ASAP) — 30 Minutes

**Once you pick a carrier:**

1. **Call to confirm you want to bind**
   - "I want to bind [policy name], can we do this today?"
   - Get verbal confirmation you're covered

2. **Request certificate of insurance**
   - Ask for PDF immediately
   - Print or save to: `/LLC Business/Insurance/`

3. **Complete application**
   - Fill out online or paper form
   - Provide: LLC name, EIN, address, drone model, usage
   - Sign where required

4. **Make first payment**
   - Full year or first month?
   - Use business bank account or credit card
   - Get receipt/confirmation number

5. **File everything**
   - Policy document: `/LLC Business/Insurance/[CARRIER]_POLICY.pdf`
   - Certificate of insurance: `/LLC Business/Insurance/[CARRIER]_CERT.pdf`
   - Receipt/payment confirmation: `/LLC Business/Insurance/[CARRIER]_RECEIPT.pdf`

| Red Flag | Why | What to Do |
|----------|-----|-----------|

| "Can't bind for 2+ weeks" | Too slow | Pick a different carrier |
