# Independent Contractor Agreement — Universal Template

**Use this for ANY contractor, ANY location, ANY service. Send via DocuSign before assigning first job. Signature required before any work or payment.**

---

## How to Use This Document

1. Fill in bracketed fields
2. Upload to DocuSign (or similar e-signature platform)
3. Send to contractor before their first job
4. Contractor must sign before you send them any job details
5. Keep signed copy in `/05_PARTNERSHIPS/Contractors/[Name]/`
6. Re-verify insurance annually (set calendar reminder)

---

## INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** [Date]

**Between:**
- **Company:** [Your LLC Name], a [State] limited liability company ("Company")
- **Contractor:** [Contractor Full Name / Business Name] ("Contractor")

---

### 1. Relationship of Parties

Contractor is retained as an **independent contractor**, not an employee, agent, or partner of Company. This Agreement does not create an employment, partnership, or joint venture relationship. Contractor:

- Sets their own hours and schedule
- Determines their own method of performing services
- Uses their own tools, equipment, and materials
- May accept or decline any job offered without penalty
- May perform services for other clients, including competitors
- Is responsible for their own business licenses, permits, and certifications
- Receives no employee benefits (health insurance, paid leave, retirement) from Company

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee relationship.

---

### 2. Scope of Services

Contractor will perform services as described in individual **Job Orders** issued by Company from time to time. Each Job Order will specify:

- Service type and description
- Location
- Deliverables required
- Deadline
- Compensation

Contractor has no obligation to accept any Job Order. Acceptance of a Job Order creates a binding commitment to complete that specific job under the terms stated.

---

### 3. Compensation

- **Rate:** As specified per Job Order, or [$___/hour, $___/job, $___/deliverable]
- **Payment Schedule:** [50% upon job confirmation, 50% upon delivery and quality approval] (or as otherwise stated in Job Order)
- **Payment Method:** [ACH / Wire / PayPal / Wise / other]
- **Currency:** [USD / local currency, specify conversion method if applicable]

Company will not withhold income taxes, Social Security, or Medicare from payments. Contractor is solely responsible for reporting and paying all applicable federal, state, local, and foreign taxes on income earned under this Agreement.

Company will issue Contractor a **Form 1099-NEC** (U.S. contractors) or equivalent tax documentation annually if required by law.

---

### 4. Insurance Requirements

Contractor shall, at their own expense, maintain throughout the term of this Agreement:

- **General/Professional Liability Insurance:** Minimum $1,000,000 per occurrence
- **Service-Specific Insurance** (as applicable): e.g., drone/aviation liability, vehicle liability, errors & omissions
- Company named as **additional insured** on the policy

Contractor must provide a **Certificate of Insurance** before accepting any Job Order. Coverage lapse voids eligibility for future Job Orders and may result in immediate termination of this Agreement.

Contractor is solely responsible for any damage, injury, or loss arising from performance of services. Company bears no liability for Contractor's acts or omissions.

---

### 5. Quality Standards & Deliverables

Contractor agrees to:

- Perform services in a professional, workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards
- Meet all deadlines specified in Job Orders
- Deliver all specified deliverables in the format and method requested
- Correct any deficient work identified during quality review, at no additional charge, within 24-48 hours of notice
- Not represent themselves as an employee, officer, or agent of Company

Failure to meet quality standards after **two (2) correction requests** may result in forfeiture of payment for that Job Order and/or termination of this Agreement.

---

### 6. Intellectual Property

All deliverables produced under this Agreement — including photographs, videos, reports, data, documents, and other work product — are considered **"work made for hire"** and are the sole property of Company upon delivery and payment.

Contractor:

- Assigns all right, title, and interest in deliverables to Company
- Waives any moral rights to the extent permitted by law
- May NOT use deliverables for self-promotion, portfolio, or resale without Company's prior written consent
- May NOT sell or license the same deliverables to any other party

---

### 7. Confidentiality

Contractor agrees to keep confidential all information about Company's clients, pricing, business methods, and proprietary systems learned during the course of this Agreement. This includes:

- Client contact information and property details
- Company pricing structure and margins
- Business processes, forms, and systems shared by Company

Contractor shall not contact Company's clients directly for future work outside this Agreement, nor solicit Company's clients for the duration of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** after termination.

---

### 8. Non-Circumvention

For **twelve (12) months** following termination of this Agreement, Contractor agrees not to directly solicit or accept work from any client introduced to Contractor through Company, without Company's written consent.

---

### 9. Indemnification

Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from:

- Contractor's negligence, misconduct, or breach of this Agreement
- Injury or property damage caused by Contractor during service performance
- Contractor's violation of any law, regulation, or third-party right

---

### 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon written notice (email acceptable). Termination does not affect:

- Payment owed for completed Job Orders
- Confidentiality obligations (survive termination)
- Non-circumvention obligations (survive termination per Section 8)
- IP assignment for prior work (survives termination)

---

### 11. Independent Business Operation

Contractor represents that they:

- Operate as an independent business (sole proprietor, LLC, or corporation)
- Are free to accept or decline work from Company and other clients
- Provide their own tools, equipment, transportation, and supplies
- Bear the risk of profit or loss on jobs undertaken
- Are not economically dependent solely on Company

---

### 12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within 30 days, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in [Your State/Country], under the rules of [American Arbitration Association or local equivalent]. Each party bears its own legal costs unless otherwise awarded.

---

### 13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [Your State], USA, without regard to conflict of law principles, except where local law at the service location mandatorily applies (e.g., local labor classification rules, aviation regulations).

---

### 14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with any Job Orders issued under it, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions or agreements, written or oral.

## Localization Notes

**This template is written for U.S. contractor law (1099 classification).** If hiring contractors outside the U.S., or in jurisdictions with strict worker-classification laws (e.g., California ABC test, EU gig-worker rules, UK IR35), have a local attorney review before use. Key risk areas:

- **California:** ABC test is strict — contractor must be free from control, perform work outside usual course of business, and be customarily engaged in an independently established trade. Drone/inspection work may qualify; verify.
- **European Union:** Many countries presume employment unless contractor has multiple clients and controls their own work — document contractor's other clients.
- **UK:** IR35 rules may reclassify contractors as employees for tax purposes if Company exercises too much control — avoid dictating exact hours/methods.
- **Canada:** Similar independent contractor tests (control, ownership of tools, chance of profit/loss).

**When expanding internationally, budget for one-time local legal review (~$500-1,500 per country) rather than assuming this U.S. template transfers directly.**
