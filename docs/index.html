Aerial job-site documentation

The PROFESSIONAL'S view of the job site.

Date-stamped, matched-angle aerial packs for general contractors and small developers in Valdosta and Lowndes County. Same vantage each visit — so a pad, a tank, or a roof line can be compared without another truck roll.

This is the work satellite and EagleView miss: new builds that aren't on a map yet, and lots under heavy South Georgia canopy. You get organized folders — still photos, a short note on what changed since last time, and a date on every frame. Qualitative change between visits. One operator, plain English, local.

What we document

  • Job-site progress from the air, same angle, date on the file
  • Folders you can hand to an owner, a lender, or your own crew
  • Sites that don't show up clean on satellite — new pads and heavy tree cover

Aerial documentation: what it is not

Capture only. We capture. Your licensed team certifies. Not a land survey. Not an inspection. Not an acreage or boundary determination. Not a stamped professional report. Not a dollar figure for a carrier. Not a takeoff or a measurement set. Not a licensed building or roof review. Photos and a plain-language note on what looks different — that's the pack.
