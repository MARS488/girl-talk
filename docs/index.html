Aerial job-site documentation

The PROFESSIONAL'S view of the job site.

Date-stamped, matched-angle aerial packs for general contractors and small developers in Valdosta and Lowndes County. Same vantage each visit — so a pad, a tank, or a roof line can be compared without another truck roll.

This is the work satellite and EagleView miss: new builds that aren't on a map yet, and lots under heavy South Georgia canopy. Aerial documentation: what it is not

Capture only. Not a land survey. Not a stamped professional report. Not a dollar figure for a carrier. Not a takeoff or a measurement set. Not a licensed building or roof review. Photos and a plain-language note on what looks different — that's the pack.
