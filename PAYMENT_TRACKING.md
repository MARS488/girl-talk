## Payment Status Key

- **✓ Paid:** Full payment received
- **⏳ Pending:** Invoice sent, awaiting payment
- **◐ Partial:** Deposit received, balance outstanding
- **🔴 Overdue:** 30+ days past due date
- **⚠️ Collections:** Multiple follow-ups required

## How to Use

1. **After every job:** Create invoice, send to client
2. **Every Friday:** Update payment tracking (check for deposits, follow up on unpaid)
3. **Every month:** Review monthly summary, total revenue, reconcile with bank
4. **Every quarter:** Calculate tax set-aside (25% of total revenue), move to separate account

---

## Quick Reference: Invoice Status Check

```
Payment Status = Total Paid / Invoice Amount

100% = ✓ Paid (close job)
50-99% = ◐ Partial (follow up for balance)
0-49% = ⏳ Pending (send reminder)
Overdue = 🔴 (escalate follow-up)
```
