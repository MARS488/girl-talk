Green flags 💚 vs red flags 🚩

A quick gut-check. This is guidance, not a diagnosis.

Green: consistent words & actions, respects a "no," makes real plans, calm during disagreements, curious about your feelings.

Red: makes you doubt your own memory, love-bombs then withdraws, isolates you from friends, monitors your phone, "jokes" that sting, escalates when you set a boundary.

Trust the pattern, not the apology. If reading this made your stomach drop, that's worth listening to.
